# v1.0 Milestone Audit

## Overview
This audit verified the `v1.0` (Prototype) milestone comprising 6 phases. All requirements have been satisfied without orphans, all phases received a `passed` status in their respective `VERIFICATION.md` files, and integration cross-flows perfectly bridge the React Frontend to the FastAPI backend and ultimately out to WhatsApp deep linking.

### Requirements Score
**23 out of 23 requirements satisfied.**

## Cross-Phase Integration & Flows
- ✅ **Frontend PWA to OCR Pipeline**: The Next.js frontend properly captures user images and sends multi-part form data to the FastAPI `/api/v1/analyze` endpoint.
- ✅ **OCR to Safety Rule Engine**: Text parsed by Google Cloud Vision maps logically against MongoDB fuzz parameters, resolving clinical verification.
- ✅ **Safety Output to WhatsApp Chatbot**: End-to-end `wa.me` deep-linking populates the user's mobile app directly from scanner diagnostics.

## Tech Debt & Deferred Items
The following non-blocking technological compromises were explicitly accepted during initial scope:
- **Phase 05 (Admin Dashboard)**: JWT tokens are stored locally inside `localStorage` for ease of PWA implementation, presenting an XSS vulnerability. This is acceptable for the PIMNAS prototype but should migrate to `httpOnly` secure cookies prior to funding distribution.
